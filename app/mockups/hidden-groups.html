Hidden groups mockup. Example collection, already set up for scenario 2: Proposal issue and Topic are hidden, and you've clicked the issue group, so the first bookmark is selected.
  1. Turn Ungrouped bookmarks on and off (new, highlighted). Off hides bookmarks that are in no shown group, in the graph and the queue.
  2. Look at the current bookmark: its hidden groups appear as peeking groups, with the bookmarks they link. Click another bookmark or group to see them come and go.
  3. Pick the peeking style you like below.

Peeking style

Filter… (F)

Which groups peek. Only the current bookmark's groups. Bookmarks in your box selection don't bring their hidden groups back, or most of them would return.

What comes with them. A peeking group brings its bookmarks, even ones Ungrouped bookmarks would hide, with dashed lines, so you can see what else is in it. They leave when you move on.

Right panel. It already lists every group of the bookmark. Groups of a hidden type get a small "hidden" marker so the panel and the graph agree.
