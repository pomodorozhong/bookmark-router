Undo and messages

One toast, one Undo button

  • Bottom bar: Undo · “Nothing to undo” · message is removed. The bottom of the graph keeps only the graph tools.
  • Undo moves to the header, next to ⋯. Its tooltip names what it undoes; ⌘Z/Ctrl Z works anywhere outside a text field. It undoes any saved change, as today.
  • Every saved change shows one dark toast at the bottom center of the graph: what happened, plus Undo. It replaces the previous toast, hides after 6 seconds, and pauses while hovered.
  • Notices such as “Backup restored” and “Copied 12 bookmarks” use the same toast without Undo. Errors use a red toast that stays until closed.
  • Queue thumbnails: each row keeps a 56 × 40 slot on the left. A bookmark's image fills it, cropped to a rounded rectangle; rows without an image keep the slot empty so titles stay aligned. The queue is wider (310 px) to make room.
  • Help is removed from the graph's bottom bar.
  • The old light toast at bottom right is gone, so nothing covers Done and Drop.
Collections

Two ways to delete a collection

  • Collections menu: a trash button appears when you hover or focus a row.
  • ⋯ menu: “Delete this collection…” at the bottom, in red.
  • Both open the same dialog: the counts, what gets removed (bubbles, tags, images, graph positions), and a Download backup button before the red Delete collection.
  • Deleting removes everything from this device. If it was the open collection, the app switches to your next collection, or to a new empty “My collection” if none is left.
